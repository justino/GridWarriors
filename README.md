## UI

* Red Square - You
* All other squares - Enemies
* Flat disc - Deadly
* Rectangle disc - Passive

## How to play

This is a keyboard based game.

### Movement Keys

* `w`: North
* `s`: South
* `a`: West
* `d`: East

You can move diagonally with combinations of the keys.

### Disc throwing

Press the spacebar to throw your disc in the direction you are facing.
You face the direction you last moved, including diagonals.

* `space`: Throw

If you have a number pad, you can also use it to aim your throw in any of the eight directions without turning.

* `8`: North
* `2`: South
* `6`: East
* `4`: West
* `7`: North West
* `9`: North East
* `1`: South West
* `3`: South East

### Deflecting

Hold to deflect, release to stop. While deflecting you can't move, but the movement keys still turn you to aim the deflector arc.
Enemy discs that hit the arc bounce off it. You need your disc in hand to deflect.

* `b` or number pad `5`: Deflect

### Pausing

You can pause the game.
While the game is paused, the game grid will be blanked out so you can't see where you or your enemies are located.

To pause the game:

* `p`: Pause

## Gameplay

Your disc is your weapon. Throw it around the board and hit your enemies to kill them.

Watch out though, your enemies have discs too!

Use your control keys to move Adora around the board. Throw your deadly disc at enemies using the spacebar or the number pad.

Recall your disc by releasing the throw key, then pressing the spacebar or any number pad throw key again.

KILL EVERYTHING THAT MOVES

## Scoring

Each enemy type in the game is worth a certain amount of points.
Killing them will earn you points.
Get hit though, and you'll lose points.

## Enemy Types

* Warrior: normal speed, bad disc accuracy, kill with one hit
* Bulldog: slow speed, better accuracy, kill with 2 hits, regenerates
* Leader: fast speed, best accuracy, harder hitting. Watch out, they have 2 different kinds of discs
* Guard: (not in game yet)
